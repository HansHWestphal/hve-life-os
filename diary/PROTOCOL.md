# Esopeira diary protocol

**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Status:** Container. Not a drill. No entry here has survived a trace.
**Owner:** Hans Westphal. Agents append only what this file allows.

## Why this shape

The function is Crowley's Liber E, section I, published in *The Equinox* I:1 (1909). Record the experiment during it, or immediately after. Note body and mind. Note time, place, weather, and any condition that might cause, assist, inhibit, or confound the result. Emotions are conditions. The more scientific the record, the better. Use your own intelligence. Do not rely on a distinguished person for what the trace does not show. *John St. John* is the form model: a running log with clock times, not a summary written the next day.

Llewellyn's later paper journal, Donald Michael Kraig's *The Magical Diary* (1990), turned that rule into fields: moon, name of the working, purpose, how it went, result later. A catalog ad in another session attributed a Commodore floppy of the same function to Gerald and Betty Schueler's Llewellyn Starwares series. No disk image or source has been found. Schueler was a systems analyst. Betty Schueler was a computer consultant. The Commodore 64 was one of the first computers Hans learned on. That is the personal line. It does not make Schueler a founder, and it does not join an order.

Crowley is a borrowed rule. Not a founder. Not an affiliation.

## What an entry is

A science log row. Conditions, what happened, what is not concluded. An operation may be entered only after a trace. Write the miss. A match is not a law.

## What an agent may prefill

- `started_at` if the session clock is known
- `conditions.sky` from the morning brief, marked as source
- `sources` when the brief or a cited page was actually used

## What an agent may not fill

- `happened`
- `not_concluded`
- `result_later`
- any conclusion the entry does not support

GitHub holds this protocol and the blank sample. Raw entries stay on hardware Hans controls, unless he extracts one.
